# Investing the effect of the Return Distribution vs Long-run compounded wealth

A simple simulation project to visually investigate the effect of the return distribution on the long-run compounded wealth holding the asset.

The long run expected growth rate is approximated with a taylor series expansion up till its second term. For small $R$, the 3rd and 4th moment may be very small and inconsequential to the overall expected log growth.

calculations.ipynb contains the Monte Carlo simulations of the return distribution and its effect on the long-term compounded wealth as measured by the 25, 50 and 75th percentiles of compounded wealth as well as the mean logarithmic growth.
